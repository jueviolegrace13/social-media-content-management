# Phase 6: Standalone app for external businesses

**Status:** Not started

## Goal

Businesses without Open Play sign up and use the social app directly.

## Scope

**In:** sign-up, dashboard, content composer, calendar (month/week/day), media library, publishing history, Instagram Business.
**Out:** AI features, analytics beyond basic post links.

## Tasks

- [ ] Self sign-up, tenant creation, plan selection
- [ ] Dashboard: connected accounts, drafts, scheduled, published, failures
- [ ] Composer: caption, media, destinations, per network preview, drafts
- [ ] Calendar: month, week and day views; drag to reschedule
- [ ] Media library: folders/tags, reuse, storage limits per plan
- [ ] Publishing history with filters
- [ ] Instagram Business adapter (image/video required, public media URL, container publish flow, daily limit)
- [ ] Team members inside a tenant (owner, editor)

## Acceptance criteria

- An external business can sign up, connect a Facebook Page and an Instagram account, and schedule posts from the calendar.

## Depends on

Phases 2 and 5.
